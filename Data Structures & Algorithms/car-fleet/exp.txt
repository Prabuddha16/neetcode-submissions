The key observation is that cars are moving toward the same target
and a faster car cannot overtake a slower car.
So if a car behind reaches the target in less time than
or equal to the car or fleet ahead, it will eventually catch that fleet
and become part of the same fleet.

I sort the cars by position in descending order
so I process them from the car closest to the target to the farthest.
For each car, I calculate its independent arrival time as (target - position) / speed.
If a car behind has an arrival time less than or equal to the fleet ahead,
it will catch that fleet because cars cannot overtake. Otherwise, it forms a new fleet.
Therefore, every time the current arrival time is greater than the previous fleet's arrival time,
I increment the fleet count.
