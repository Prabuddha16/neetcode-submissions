Entrance

"Duplicate creates a cycle; slow and fast meet inside it;
reset one pointer; where they meet again is the duplicate."

"I treat the array like a linked list, where nums[index] tells me the next position.
Because one number is repeated, a cycle is created.
I use two pointers: slow moves one step and fast moves two steps.
First, they meet inside the cycle.
Then I move slow back to the starting point and move both pointers one step at a time.
Where they meet again is the duplicate number."
